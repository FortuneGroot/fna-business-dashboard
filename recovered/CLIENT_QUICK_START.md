1. Open **Dashboard** → Needs Attention  
2. Open **Opportunities** → Unassigned + stage **01 New Lead**

4. Open **Conversations** and answer people who wrote you  
5. Check **Calendars** for today / tomorrow

## Find a customer

**Contacts** → search name, email, or phone  
or click their name on the sales board

---

## Sales board (left to right)

New Lead → Attempting Contact → Connected → Qualified → Appointment Booked → Appointment Completed → Estimate Sent → Follow-Up → Decision Pending → Won / Lost  

Long-Term Nurture is a parking place — not this week’s forecast.

You mark **Qualified, Estimate, Decision, Won, Lost**.  
A real calendar booking can mark **Appointment Booked**.  
**Won** is not the same as money in the bank.

## Website forms (what they do)

Your site may use contact, estimate, appointment-request, feedback, and qualification forms.  
Feedback is private and does **not** create a sales deal.  
Public review links are optional and honest — not gated by a score.

## When a human must take over

They ask for a person, they are upset, you don’t know the price, it’s a current customer with a problem, or the system flagged Needs Attention / human follow-up.

## What you should NOT change

Please don’t change these without asking Fortune & Automations:

- Workflows / automations (do not Publish)  
- Forms connected to the website  
- Funnel / page publish settings  
- Pipeline stages  
- Phone numbers / sending email setup  
- Triggers for missed call, review ask, or AI  
- Permission / consent fields on customer records “to make texts work”  
- Dashboard widgets Fortune & Automations built for you  

It’s OK to: reply to conversations, add notes, move opportunities, update contact info, complete tasks.

---

## Never

- Publish / turn on workflows yourself  
- Turn on AI  
- Delete **do-not-automate** or similar protection tags  
- Invent a price in a text  
- Merge two customers just because they share a phone  
- Treat Won dollars as cash in the bank  
- Install or recommend a third-party CRM mobile app for this system
